Meet the dogs

One chirpy cavapoo, one dramatic Samusky, and a shared commitment to getting the music news right.

Winnie

Apricot cavapoo · hooks the story

Winnie is a girl, all apricot and cream curls, with a bright, chirpy, playful British voice and pink subtitles. She does the intros and the hooks. If a new album is out this Friday, you’ll hear her first — usually at slightly too much volume, and with absolutely no apology.

Koda

Samusky · facts, then “paws down”

Koda is a boy: a grey and white Samusky, which is a Samoyed with a dash of husky, and a professional drama dog. His voice is a slightly higher-pitched British male — expressive, not manly or deadpan — with husky-style woos and the odd little howl when the news deserves it. He keeps the facts straight, then signs off: paws down.

The short, factual version

Winnie and Koda are real dogs who live with Chris Faulkes in the UK. They star in short talking-dog videos about current, verified music news. The photographs on this site are real photographs of the dogs and have not been edited to change their markings, limbs or faces. The talking videos use generated voices, not cloned ones, and should be labelled as AI-generated when they are posted. Every news story here links back to its source.

Frequently asked questions

The practical stuff, in plain language. The dogs would like it noted that they also accept biscuits.

Who are Winnie and Koda?

Winnie is an apricot and cream cavapoo, a girl with a bright, chirpy British voice and pink subtitles. Koda is a grey and white Samusky — a Samoyed with some husky — a boy and a bit of a drama dog, with a slightly higher-pitched British voice, teal subtitles, and the odd woo or howl. They are real dogs, and they star in short talking videos about music news.

What does “Paws down” mean?

Paws down is the brand tagline and Koda’s sign-off at the end of every video. Think of it as their version of “case closed” — the news has been delivered, the drama has had its moment, and that’s the end of the story.

What do they talk about?

Music news: new releases, charts, and new vinyl, CD and cassette releases. Every story on this site is taken from a linked source. They don’t invent chart positions, prices or rarity claims.

Are the talking videos real voices?

The dogs are real, and the photographs are real. The talking videos use generated voices, not cloned ones, and should be labelled as AI-generated when they are posted. Winnie’s voice is a bright, chirpy, playful British female. Koda’s is a slightly higher-pitched, dramatic British male, with husky-style woos and little howls.
